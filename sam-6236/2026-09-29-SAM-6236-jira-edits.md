## SAM-6240 reshape

- New title: "[In-App] Mobile SDK enabler: app connection, triggers, overlay display and offline rules".
- Added the phone-side work no story owned. New requirements are numbered SDK-1 to SDK-17 and NFR-SDK-1 so they don't clash with PRD IDs:
  - Connect the app and send its screen list.
  - Send the app version.
  - Register test devices by QR code.
  - Trigger timing: Next App Open, contextual events, trigger delay, in-session spacing.
  - Display-rule order.
  - Rendering the 5 formats: safe area, close button, text scaling, dimming, screen reader.
  - How each way of closing maps to an event.
  - An opt-out call for the client app.
- Explicit non-goal: no template registration for Custom HTML.
- P0 kept at 8. Two new P0s: app connection, and close method to event. Two old P0s moved to P1 Test Cases: clock change (SAM-6290) and malformed event (SAM-6291).
- Carried over from SAM-5465: the screen-reader focus rule and "a malformed campaign never crashes the app".
- 5 open questions listed in the ticket.
